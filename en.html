01My profile

From market data to strategic implications.

I have worked on the engineering, business and consulting sides: from designing solar plants to off-grid storage systems in Africa, from PPAs for industry to managing energy across 21 company offices. I combine market data, regulatory analysis and business models to spot opportunities, assess risks and support decisions.
